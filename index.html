Campaign dossier

Ten missions · One platoon · Every man remembered

Three-act campaign

Act I — The landing (Missions 1–3): A scattered platoon gathers in the flooded fields, secures a crossroads, then escorts medics through a besieged village.

Act II — The long push (Missions 4–7): Ambushes, a night bridge demolition, and a costly armor duel test the bonds between Baker's veterans and replacements.

Act III — The last mile (Missions 8–10): In the Falaise pocket, the company chooses who gets out, breaks through, and reaches a quiet farm where survivors read the names of the missing.

Field manual

  • Touch controls: tap a squad to select; choose Move, Attack, or Suppress, then tap destination/target. Tactical pause lets you queue decisions.
  • Cover: squads snap to hedges, walls, and craters. Green is strong, yellow partial, red exposed. Approach from the protected side; flanking fire weakens protection.
  • Suppression: fire builds suppression; pinned squads lose accuracy and cannot advance well. Focus fire or use suppress, then flank defenders.
  • Persistence: surviving squads gain veterancy and gear; wounded return with reduced strength. Fallen soldiers are permanently remembered. 12-minute missions have checkpoints.
  • Tech / business: Unity URP, NavMesh + cover-point graph, squad FSM, influence-map fog of war, deterministic combat. Free first chapter, fair full-game unlock, optional cosmetics only; no pay-to-win.
Story is delivered through illustrated field-journal panels, in-engine events, radio chatter, and letters written by soldiers between missions.
